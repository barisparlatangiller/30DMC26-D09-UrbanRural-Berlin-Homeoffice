Method and limits

Empty homes. Zensus 2022 (Destatis), 1 km grid: dwellings × vacancy rate. "Available on the market" uses the market-active vacancy rate (empty and available within about 3 months). Where Destatis suppresses a rate for privacy, the cell counts no empty homes, so totals are a floor.

Time to central Berlin. VBB timetable (GTFS) for a normal Tuesday: the latest departure from each station that still arrives at Berlin Hauptbahnhof, Friedrichstraße, Alexanderplatz, Potsdamer Platz or Zoologischer Garten by 9:00 (3 minutes per change). Getting to the station: walking up to 1.2 km, otherwise 18 km/h by bike, bus or car plus 4 minutes.

Scenario. Office days = 5 − home-office days. Monthly commuting = 2 trips × door-to-centre time × office days × 4.33 weeks. A cell qualifies when that stays within the hours you accept. With 5 home-office days, every place qualifies.

Limits. No single empty house can be identified. An empty dwelling is not automatically for rent or sale, and some are empty because they are run down. This is a scenario, not a forecast of migration or a fix for the housing crisis.
